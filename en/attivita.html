Our activities

Six areas, one common thread.

Our team's experience and multidisciplinary set-up, together with the cohesion of the whole working group, are our best guarantee of competence and professionalism.

01

Ichthyology and fish fauna management

Where we started: knowing fish in order to protect and manage them.

  • Ichthyology
  • Fish maps and fisheries management plans
  • Aquaculture, hatcheries and fish farming
  • Management of recreational and commercial fishing

02

Environmental monitoring and assessment

Field data read with rigour, to understand the state of rivers and lakes and for assessment procedures.

  • Applied ecology and biomonitoring
  • Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)
  • Appropriate assessment (Habitats Directive)
  • Strategic Environmental Assessment (SEA)
  • Integrated Environmental Authorisation (IEA)
  • Environmental modelling
  • Urban runoff management
  • Vegetation impact studies

03

Restoration, bioengineering and land management

Works that give space and ecological function back to water and land, also with BLU Progetti.

  • River and lake restoration
  • Soil and water bioengineering
  • Constructed wetlands and lagooning
  • Ecological networks
  • Management and conservation of high-altitude ecosystems
  • Design and works supervision for forest restoration and reforestation
  • Forest resource management
  • Landscape and green space design
  • Agriculture

04

Fish passes and hydropower

Where water meets a dam or a weir, fish must be able to get through.

  • Fish passes
  • Video monitoring of fish migration in fish passes
  • Sediment management in reservoirs

05

LIFE programmes and European research

Projects co-funded by the European Union for species and habitats, from LIFE to Interreg and the Italian recovery plan.

  • LIFE Programme
  • LIFE PREDATOR project
  • Interreg cooperation programme (Sharesalmo)
  • ECO4TICINO
  • ProSpIttiCo

06

Education and outreach

Telling the story of water to those who live by it: schools, citizens, anglers.

  • Environmental education and outreach
